Proposal: jobs activity area (not in Figma yet)

  1. At a glance. A new Activity button sits left of Help. While jobs run, a progress ring circles it and the badge counts running jobs.
  2. Attention. An amber dot marks finished jobs that need review or ended with errors. It clears when you open the panel.
  3. Summary. The panel lists running jobs, waiting jobs, and jobs that finished in the last 24 hours (at most 10).
  4. Navigation. Each row opens that job's detail page. "View all jobs" opens the Jobs page.
  5. Truth rules. Updates arrive live. Held jobs never promise a start time. Unknown outcomes say "Needs review", never success.
  6. Record. The panel is a summary. The Jobs page and job detail stay the durable record.
